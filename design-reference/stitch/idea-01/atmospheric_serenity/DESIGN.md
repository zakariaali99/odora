## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet architectural luxury, sensory wellness, and modern domestic tranquility. Crafted for a smart fragrance ecosystem where high technology seamlessly integrates into elevated living spaces, the interface prioritizes stillness, warm tactile textures, and unhurried interactions over typical smart-device utilitarianism.

The design movement bridges **Warm Organic Minimalism** and **Tactile Neomorphism**. Visually, the system evokes the calming presence of travertine stone, matte ceramic diffusers, linen fabric, and botanical foliage. Interfaces feel like physical, hand-finished interior objects rather than cold digital screens. High amounts of breathing room, subtle tonal layering, soft radius curves, and precise typographic hierarchy create an experience that feels meditative, refined, and effortlessly prestigious.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in natural mineral tones, botanical herbals, and raw linen textures:

- **Primary (`#919C7A`)**: A desaturated, earthy sage green inspired by dried leaves and matte ceramic hardware. It acts as the primary accent for active states, key interactive indicators, and primary action surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#E1F2BD`)**: A luminous, soft sprout green that provides vibrant, fresh illumination for active toggle badges, highlights, low-intensity ambient glows, and active mist states.
- **Tertiary (`#2D3329`)**: A grounded, deep charcoal olive serving as high-contrast body typography, structural iconography, and primary buttons that require decisive visual anchoring.
- **Neutral (`#F4F0EC`)**: A warm, tactile off-white/cream canvas evoking limestone, Japanese washi paper, and unglazed porcelain. 

### Surface Roles & Neutral Palette
- **Canvas Base**: `#F4F0EC` (Warm off-white base)
- **Card / Panel Surface**: `#FFFFFF` with 70–95% opacity or pure matte `#FFFFFF` to lift content subtly from the canvas.
- **Subtle Stroke & Hairline**: `#EAE5DF` (Soft warm sand border)
- **Muted Text / Secondary Labels**: `#70756B` (Mid-tone sage slate, providing accessible contrast while remaining calm)
- **Deep Accent / Pure Shadow Tint**: `#1E221B` (Deepest forest charcoal)

## Typography

The typographic treatment marries geometric architectural construction with ergonomic digital readability:

- **Headlines & Display (`Space Grotesk`)**: Echoes the circular geometry and structured rhythm of the Geomini aesthetic found in the brand identity. Set with balanced letterforms and tight negative tracking in larger scales, it conveys contemporary industrial elegance.
- **Body & Microcopy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers warm, balanced letter shapes with open apertures, ensuring effortless legibility across scent descriptions, schedules, device pairing workflows, and technical details.
- **Eyebrows & Technical Labels**: Rendered in uppercase `Space Grotesk` with expanded letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) to function like quiet, architectural room markers or engraved packaging typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows an expansive, curated gallery layout. White space is treated as an active luxury material rather than void space. Content breathes freely, mimicking the physical dispersion of delicate aroma mist in a sunlit room.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapting Form Factors
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column symmetrical fluid grid with a maximum content container of 1280px. Gutters set at `1.5rem` (`24px`) and exterior outer canvas margins set at `3rem` (`48px`) to `4rem` (`64px`). Single modules span deliberate wide blocks (typically 4, 6, or 8 columns).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout, maintaining `1.5rem` gutters with `2rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins. Smart home device control interfaces adopt full-width stacked touch cards with generous vertical padding to prevent mis-taps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth eschews harsh artificial drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering**, **Warm Ambient Diffusion**, and **Low-Contrast Outlines**:

- **Subtle Surface Lift (Level 1 - Cards & Control Sheets)**: Layered using an ultra-fine border (`1px solid #EAE5DF`) resting on pure `#FFFFFF` or a semi-translucent `#FFFFFF` (94% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). Ambient elevation is cast with a diffused, herbal-tinted warmth: `0 8px 30px rgba(45, 51, 41, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Controls & Primary Toggles (Level 2)**: Sliders, active power buttons, and floating navigation pills receive soft, dual-tinted atmospheric depth: `0 12px 36px rgba(45, 51, 41, 0.07), 0 2px 6px rgba(45, 51, 41, 0.03)`.
- **Recessed / Inset Wells (Trackers & Sliders)**: Horizontal fragrance intensity sliders and schedule bars use an inset track look: `inset 0 1px 3px rgba(45, 51, 41, 0.08)` filled with `#EFEAE4`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by the soft, cylindrical curves of the physical diffuser hardware. Corners are systematically rounded (`roundedness: 2`, with base radii of `0.5rem` / `8px` up to `1.5rem` / `24px` on interactive containers).

- **Hardware Cards & Controls**: Rounded at `1.25rem` (`20px`) to `1.5rem` (`24px`), reflecting the smooth organic rim of the diffuser unit.
- **Chips, Scrubbers & Pills**: Fully rounded pill capsules (`9999px`) used for mist level indicators, scent category filters, and touch toggles.
- **Inputs & Modal Surfaces**: Curvature harmonized at `0.75rem` (`12px`) to `1rem` (`16px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Power / Master Action**: Deep charcoal tertiary (`#2D3329`) pill or soft-corner rectangle with off-white text (`#F4F0EC`). Hover transitions subtly darken to `#1E221B` with a soft scale transform (`scale(0.995)`).
- **Secondary Tone**: Solid sage `#919C7A` background with crisp white or dark charcoal iconography.
- **Ghost / Neutral**: `#FFFFFF` surface with a `1px solid #EAE5DF` perimeter, transitioning on hover to `#F4F0EC`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Capsule Chips**: Used for room selection (e.g., "Living Room", "Master Suite") and scent profiles (e.g., "Forest Sage", "Cotton Linen").
- **Inactive**: Transparent or `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #EAE5DF` outline, `#70756B` typography.
- **Active**: Filled with `#E1F2BD` (soft sprout green) paired with `#2D3329` label, providing clear, gentle state confirmation without visual aggression.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Flat, clean `#FFFFFF` field bounded by `#EAE5DF`. Focus state transitions border to `#919C7A` with a delicate `0 0 0 3px rgba(145, 156, 122, 0.2)` focus ring.
- **Fragrance Intensity Sliders**: Continuous pill track (`8px` height) in `#EFEAE4` with active sage progress fill (`#919C7A`). The drag thumb is a tactile white circular puck (`24px`) cast with an ambient shadow.

### Cards & Control Panels
- **Device Status Cards**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, delicate border, spacious internal padding (`1.5rem`). Header presents live status (e.g., "Diffusing", "Idle", "Cartridge 82%").
- **Cartridge / Fragrance Cards**: Feature high-contrast product photography isolated on light cream backdrops with micro-capsule indicators for top, heart, and base olfactory notes.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Navigation Bars**: Floating bottom or fixed top glass panel (`rgba(244, 240, 236, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), anchored by hairline divider `#EAE5DF`.
- **List Items**: Clean horizontal rows divided by light sand hairlines with chevron indicators (`#919C7A`) and right-aligned status values.
