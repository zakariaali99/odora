## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an intimate, sensory-rich sanctuary for nighttime relaxation and connected home fragrance rituals. It addresses discerning individuals seeking a nocturnal ritual that calms circadian rhythms, blending organic botanical sensuality with high-end device control. 

The aesthetic style merges **Dark Mode Botanical Luxury** with **Subtle Organic Glassmorphism**. The interface behaves like smooth river stones bathed in soft moonlight—warm glowing embers, translucent obsidian layers, and quiet, deliberate interactions. Surfaces feel touchable and velvet-soft rather than clinically digital, avoiding harsh contrasts in favor of glowing luminance, twilight depths, and tactile feedback.

## Colors

The color palette is built around the atmosphere of a secluded botanical conservatory at midnight.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#121613`)**: Obsidian Moss. Absorbs peripheral light, establishing low photoreceptive strain for circadian winding down.
- **Surface Low (`#181c19`)**: Nocturnal Charcoal. Base layer for recessed regions, bottom sheets, and inactive backdrops.
- **Surface Container (`#1f2420`)**: Elevated moss-slate for interactive tiles, scent capsules, and ambient mode cards.
- **Surface High (`#272d28`)**: Prominent floating panels, active popovers, and navigation surfaces.

### Accents & Illumination
- **Primary — Luminous Sage (`#a3b88c`)**: Core action states, primary switches, level indicators, and mist intensity rings.
- **Secondary — Muted Amber Gold (`#d4af7a`)**: Ambient warmth, nightlight intensity, oil depletion warnings, and schedule markers.
- **Tertiary Accent — Soft Jade & Chartreuse (`#b9caa5`, `#d2e3b8`)**: Highlights, scent profile tags, and botanical freshness metrics.
- **Warm Flame Highlight (`#e09e67`)**: Reserved for twilight wind-down timers and active diffusion heat elements.

### Text & Outlines
- **Primary Text (`#f4f0ec`)**: Soft Ivory Alabaster. Uncompromised legibility without harsh glare.
- **Secondary Text (`#c8c5c0`)**: Muted Sand. Used for secondary readings, scheduling details, and subtitles.
- **Tertiary / Subdued (`#8e948c`)**: Subdued Slate for structural labels, timestamps, and resting state icons.
- **Borders & Dividers**: `rgba(163, 184, 140, 0.18)` for fine botanical hairline borders, and `#2c352e` for structural containers.

## Typography

The type system pairs modern geometric balance with refined editorial breathing room. Outfit provides open apertures and circular geometric proportions that complement the pebble-like shape language of the hardware.

- **Weight Nuance**: Headlines use lighter weights (`300` and `400`) at display scales to preserve an airy, tranquil sanctuary aesthetic. Avoid dense black or extra-bold weights.
- **Letter Spacing**: Generous tracking is applied on small labels and scent notes to evoke high-end perfumery flacons, while larger display sizes employ slight negative tracking for composure.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture prioritizes breathing space, reducing visual clutter to calm the nervous system during evening operation.

- **Grid Architecture**: 4 columns on mobile, 8 columns on tablet, and 12 columns on desktop. Fluid horizontal margins anchor interface components within safe interaction reaches.
- **Device Scaling**: 
  - Mobile (compact viewports up to 599px) relies on centered thumb-reachable arcs, 1.25rem horizontal gutters, and high vertical padding (`space-xl`) between distinct sensory zones.
  - Tablet/Desktop viewports (600px+) present asymmetric split cards, mirroring physical diffuser placement on the left and scent management/schedules on the right.
- **Rhythm**: Internal card padding adheres strictly to `space-md` (compact widgets) and `space-lg` (primary atmospheric controls).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses luminous diffusion rather than mechanical drop shadows.

- **Tonal Tiers**: Depth is expressed primarily through background elevation steps (`#121613` → `#181c19` → `#1f2420` → `#272d28`).
- **Diffuse Backlight & Halos**: Rather than cast drop shadows, active elements project a multi-stop ambient aura:
  - Mist Active Glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(163, 184, 140, 0.16)`
  - Candlelight Warmth Glow: `0 8px 36px -4px rgba(212, 175, 122, 0.20)`
- **Glassmorphic Shields**: Floating controls employ frosted glass layers with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` combined with a fine border of `1px solid rgba(163, 184, 140, 0.14)`.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines**: Non-interactive containers utilize dark moss outlines (`#2c352e`) to retain subtle structure without severing background immersion.

## Shapes

The shape system is inspired by river stones, smooth ceramic diffuser domes, and organic perfume vials. 

- **Pill System**: Buttons, scent toggles, and status badges take full pill curvature (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Pebble Containers**: Outer cards, interactive panels, and ambient sliders use ultra-soft corners (`rounded-xl` at 2rem to 3rem), evoking polished basalt and hand-turned stoneware.
- **Tactile Cavities**: Recessed slider tracks and unselected cartridge slots use inverted pill insets, reinforcing physical device intimacy.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill**: Background of Luminous Sage (`#a3b88c`) with Obsidian Moss text (`#121613`), height 52px, text `label-lg`. On press, dims slightly with an inner ambient glow of `rgba(210, 227, 184, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary Ambient**: Border of `rgba(163, 184, 140, 0.25)` over Surface Container (`#1f2420`), text in Soft Ivory Alabaster (`#f4f0ec`).
- **Ghost Evening**: Zero background, Subdued Slate (`#8e948c`) text shifting to Ivory on interaction.

### Chips & Scent Tags
- Fully rounded pills (`height: 36px`) for notes like "Atlas Cedar", "Smoked Vetiver", or "Night Lavender".
- Unselected: Surface Low (`#181c19`) with `rgba(163, 184, 140, 0.15)` border and Muted Sand (`#c8c5c0`) typography.
- Selected: Subtle gradient from `#1f2420` to `#272d28` bordered with `#a3b88c`, accompanied by a 6px glowing indicator dot.

### Sliders (Mist & Amber Glow)
- Thick, pebble-rounded tactile tracks (`height: 24px`) in `#181c19`.
- Active fill: Luminous Sage or Amber Gold gradient.
- Thumb: 32px floating ivory pebble with an ultra-soft amber or sage halo.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed in continuous capsule silhouettes (`height: 52px`) with subtle `#2c352e` perimeter framing.
- Inactive state utilizes Surface Low; active state reveals an illuminated border in `#a3b88c` with 12px blur aura.

### Cards
- Layered in Surface Container (`#1f2420`) with 2rem corner curvature.
- Rimmed with a hairline top-to-bottom subtle gradient border (`rgba(163, 184, 140, 0.2)` fading to `rgba(44, 53, 46, 0.4)`).
- Interior padding set to `space-lg`.

### Diffuser Ring Control (Custom Component)
- Central circular/elliptical dial visualizing remaining oil, mist output cadence, and LED warmth level.
- Concentric micro-lines in `rgba(163, 184, 140, 0.12)` pulsing gently to reflect the actual rhythm of the physical diffuser.
