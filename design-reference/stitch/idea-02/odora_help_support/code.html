Ultrasonic diffusers utilize water basins and heat cavitation that dilute botanicals and alter delicate molecular top notes. Odora's cold-air venturi technology atomizes undiluted 100% pure botanical oil into micro-droplets under 1 micron, preserving the complete therapeutic terpene matrix without moisture or residue on fine furniture.
Odora transducers require an exact kinematic viscosity curve (14–18 cSt). Unrefined or viscous carrier oils (e.g., jojoba, coconut) will permanently clog the micro-orifice and void the 2-Year Architectural Warranty. For non-Odora pure essences, please utilize the Odora Lab Refillable Glass Core with calibrated solvent blend.
The internal optical sensor computes exact ml/hour vaporization against your personalized scheduling preset. When the reservoir dips beneath 15%, replacement sealed glass cartridges are dispatched automatically carbon-neutral to your residence before the reservoir empties.
Our guarantee covers complete white-glove hardware replacement, micro-pump failure, internal BLE transceiver diagnostics, and free annual transducer restoration. Should performance deviate from factory acoustics (<19 dB), a complimentary exchange is initiated within 48 hours.
