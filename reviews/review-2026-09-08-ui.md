## Findings (most severe first)
| # | Sev | Finding |
|---|-----|---------|
| 1 | 🔴 **Bug (crash)** | **`/fragrances` white-screens.** `pages/content/FragranceLibraryPage.jsx` uses `useEffect` but imports only `useState` → `ReferenceError: useEffect is not defined`. The vite build does NOT catch this. **Only this page** is affected (scanned all pages/components — no other missing-hook imports). One-line fix. |
| 2 | 🟠 **Design** | **Product images are placeholders.** Every product (shop cards, product detail hero, home hero) shows the **same `design-reference` moodboard collage**, not real product photos. Biggest visual gap — the elevation plan needs real product/lifestyle imagery. |
| 3 | 🟠 **Robustness** | **No error boundary around routes.** A single component crash (#1) blanks the entire app. `components/common/ErrorBoundary.jsx` exists but isn't wrapping `<Routes>`. Wrap it so one bad page doesn't kill the whole store. |
| 4 | 🟢 Minor | Home hero uses the moodboard image too (same as #2). Consider a real hero shot. |

## Verdict
The store is close to shippable UI-wise. Fix the `/fragrances` crash, add the error boundary, and swap in real product photography — then it's genuinely premium. Fix-plan: `plans/claude plans/fix-2026-09-08-frontend-ui.md`.
